A longitudinal interview project

What's love?

What do you think love is, if you have never felt it? And what will you think once you have?

The project

Asking people before, and after

I am interviewing people who have never been in romantic love about how they imagine it: what love is, what heartbreak is, and what a relationship asks of the people in it.

Then I stay in touch. If and when a participant falls in love, I interview them again and set the two videos side by side. The question is simple: what changes in how a person talks about love once they have been in it, and what turns out to have been right all along?

How it works

Two interviews, possibly years apart

  1. The first interviewRecorded on video, with someone who has never been in romantic love. We talk about how they think about love, heartbreak and relationships.
  2. Staying in touchThere is no deadline. Some people may fall in love within a year, others may take much longer, and some may not during the project.
  3. The second interviewIf and when they fall in love, we sit down again and revisit the same ground. Then the two videos are compared.

The conversation

What I ask about

  • How do you imagine romantic love?
  • What do you imagine heartbreak to feel like?
  • What do you think is the meaning of love?
  • Where did your ideas about love come from?
  • What are your fears about love?
